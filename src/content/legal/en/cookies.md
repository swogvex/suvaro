## Short version

Visitors get no cookies. There are no advertising, analytics or third-party cookies, so there is no cookie banner.

## What we store in your browser

| Name                 | Type              | Purpose                                           | How long                          |
| -------------------- | ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Session cookie       | Cookie, technical | Keeps you signed in after you sign in             | Until you sign out or it expires  |
| `suvaro-mode`        | Local storage     | Remembers the light, dark or automatic theme      | Until you clear your browser data |
| `suvaro-lang-notice` | Local storage     | Remembers that you closed the language suggestion | Until you clear your browser data |

These items are strictly necessary or remember a choice you made, so they do not need consent under Spain's LSSI-CE.

## Visit statistics

Public pages count visits with Vercel Web Analytics and measure loading speed with Vercel Speed Insights. Neither uses cookies or identifies you.

## How to remove them

You can delete cookies and local storage at any time from your browser settings. If you delete the session cookie, you will need to sign in again.
