## The service

Suvaro is a free web app to note your own income and expenses by hand. It is offered by swogvex as a personal, non-commercial project.

## Personal use

You may use Suvaro for your own personal finances. You must be 18 or older. Keep your devices and email safe, because they are how you sign in.

## Your numbers

You type every number yourself. The numbers have no monetary value and Suvaro never moves money. Suvaro is not a bank or a financial service and nothing it shows is financial advice.

## No warranty

Suvaro is provided as it is, without warranties. We do our best to keep it working and your data safe, but we cannot promise that it will never fail. Download your data from time to time if it matters to you.

## Inactive accounts

Accounts with no activity for 30 days are deleted with all their data. We email you after 25 and after 29 days without activity, before that happens.

## Ending

You can delete your account whenever you want. We may close accounts that misuse the service or try to harm it.

## Changes

We may change these terms. We will announce changes on this website before they apply.
